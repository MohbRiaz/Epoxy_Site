Which resin should a beginner buy?

Start with the Clear 2:1 Art Resin in a small size (150 g or 300 g) and a mica pigment pack. It's forgiving, gives you up to 45 minutes to work and cures in 24 hours.

Why is my resin sticky or bendy after curing?

Usually the ratio is off or it wasn't mixed long enough. Measure 2 parts resin to 1 part hardener by weight on a digital scale, then stir slowly for 2–3 minutes, scraping the sides and bottom. [Add any other tips you give customers.]

How do I avoid bubbles?

Stir slowly rather than whipping the mix, and scrape the sides and bottom of the cup. The self-levelling formula then spreads and settles on its own. [Add your heat-gun or torch tip here.]

Will it turn yellow over time?

SuperFirst resin cures to a hard, crystal-clear surface that resists yellowing, and the Pro Epoxy Top Coat is UV stable for extra protection on pieces that sit in sunlight.
